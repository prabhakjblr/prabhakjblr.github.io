why we exist

we don't need another cafe, another boring glossy space where fancy adjectives hide unispired food and drink. where community is just a hollow word to get more footfall.
what we need is a space that's truly our's - where we can meet, bond, make connections, do deep work, have fun and truly huddle - over great coffee and simple food. a place where we can live our lives and explore ourselves

our menu

we serve single‑origin speciality coffee sourced from the lush estates of chikmagalur. Our ingredients are premium and thoughtfully chosen — our breads come from one of Bengaluru’s finest artisanal bakeries. we use high‑quality cheese, syrups, matcha, and produce. clean, honest ingredients and simple recipes come together to create delicious food and drinks.
